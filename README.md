## Write-up format

Where appropriate, each write-up will cover:

1. **Objective and scope** — what the exercise involved.
2. **Enumeration** — how relevant systems and services were identified.
3. **Analysis** — how potential vulnerabilities were assessed.
4. **Testing** — actions taken within the authorised lab and their results.
5. **Remediation** — ways the identified weaknesses could be addressed.
6. **Lessons learned** — key takeaways and improvements for future testing.

## Publication policy

I will only publish material that the relevant platform permits me to share. This includes respecting Hack The Box and other platforms' rules concerning active machines, Academy exercises, proprietary training material, solutions, and flags. Any findings involving real systems will be shared only with appropriate authorisation.

The content is for educational purposes and authorised security testing only.
